# Create your first Residential proxy

> Set up your first Bright Data Residential proxy: sign in, create a proxy in the Control Panel, verify your account and earn $5 credit for first-time setup.

<Callout icon="badge-dollar" color="#4180f9" iconType="solid">
  **Verify your account and get \$5 credit**

  Add a payment method to verify your account and receive a \$5 credit. You won’t be charged at this stage.
</Callout>

<Warning>
  **Residential proxies require a KYC-verified business account.** Access is granted only after the Bright Data compliance team reviews and approves your KYC, so a new Residential zone stays disabled until then. Start at [KYC verification](https://brightdata.com/cp/kyc) and see the [Residential network access policy](/proxy-networks/residential/network-access). No KYC yet? Use [ISP proxies](/proxy-networks/isp/introduction) or [Datacenter proxies](/proxy-networks/data-center/introduction), which need no KYC.
</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title={<a href="https://brightdata.com/cp/start">Sign in to Bright Data</a>} />

  <Step title={<>Open the <a href="https://brightdata.com/cp/zones/new">Create Proxy</a> page</>}>
    Navigate to **Create Proxy** under the **Proxies** section in the left menu.

    <Frame>
      <img src="https://mintcdn.com/brightdata/8FBihMtdCDBVIPQS/images/proxy-networks/residential/quickstart/create-proxy.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=8FBihMtdCDBVIPQS&q=85&s=e2f0d5cc621a56b51c06ff82c9a6eb9c" alt="Create Proxy" width="1784" height="990" data-path="images/proxy-networks/residential/quickstart/create-proxy.png" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose Residential Proxy">
    Select **Residential Proxy** and click **Continue**.

    <Frame>
      <img src="https://mintcdn.com/brightdata/8FBihMtdCDBVIPQS/images/proxy-networks/residential/quickstart/select-residential-proxy.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=8FBihMtdCDBVIPQS&q=85&s=2374aeb4a3ecb8f806cb147ba8042d4a" alt="Residential Proxy" width="3260" height="1420" data-path="images/proxy-networks/residential/quickstart/select-residential-proxy.png" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Assign a name to your proxy">
    Enter a meaningful name. Optionally, add a short description.

    <Warning>
      **The proxy name cannot be changed once created.**

      All other settings can be modified later. For details, see [Configure your Proxy](/proxy-networks/residential/configure-your-proxy).
    </Warning>

    <Frame>
      <img src="https://mintcdn.com/brightdata/8FBihMtdCDBVIPQS/images/proxy-networks/residential/quickstart/config-residential-proxy.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=8FBihMtdCDBVIPQS&q=85&s=334668f130b696ca9b12efd9b5ebf317" alt="Proxy Name" width="3200" height="1360" data-path="images/proxy-networks/residential/quickstart/config-residential-proxy.png" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add payment method (free trial users only)">
    If you signed up with a personal email (e.g., Gmail, GitHub) and have not yet added a payment method, the primary button will read **Add payment method** instead of **Create Proxy**.

    Click the button to open the payment verification form and complete the process. You will **not** be charged, this is for identity verification only.

    <Info>
      Already have a payment method on file? This step is skipped automatically. For full details, see [Payment Verification](/general/account/billing-and-pricing/payment-verification).
    </Info>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Finalize proxy creation">
    Click **Create Proxy**. If you were redirected to add a payment method, your zone is created automatically once verification completes. Your proxy is now ready to use.
  </Step>
</Steps>
