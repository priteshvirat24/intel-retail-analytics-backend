Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) - Laptops


1. Name some brands that offer laptops online?

Laptops from brands such as HP, Dell, Lenovo, Acer, Apple, and others are available online.

2. What are some of the key considerations before buying a laptop?

You need to evaluate your needs, such as whether you'll use the laptop for just a few hours, all day, or while traveling. A regular laptop's features differ from those of a gaming laptop.

3. What would be the ideal screen size of a laptop for students?

A convenient size for a laptop is a display screen between 33.02 cm (13 inches) and 35.56 cm (14 inches). These sizes are easy to carry between school or college and home.

4. What is the price of a laptop that offers 4K resolutions?

This depends entirely on the brand, but the laptop price is likely on the higher side.

5. How much RAM is good for a laptop?

For basic computing needs such as web browsing and document creation, 4GB or 8GB is usually sufficient. For more intensive tasks such as gaming or video editing, however, 16GB or even 32GB of RAM may be necessary.
