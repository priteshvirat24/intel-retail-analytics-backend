So since the iPad Pro release I've been on a mission to find a good stand to simple prop the iPad up a bit for sketching.  I'm a product designer by trade so I'm not doing insanely intricate work (mostly concept sketching) and after years of sketching a Cintiq, I'm used to sketching at somewhat of an angle.  I originally purchased a stand that was marketed as an iPad Pro/Macbook Air Stand from a much more expensive (almost triple the cost) and well-known brand, but it fell short in more ways than one.  THIS, however, is perfect for iPad Pro sketching.  It works so well that in fact, I'm going to also write the seller to make sure they add it to the description.
The problem with the "other" stand(s) is they simply aren't portable.  Why would you want to buy a huge clunky stand (yes it might be pretty and textured aluminum and Apple make it more appealing by offering it in their online store) and not be able to just throw it in your bag?  The Steklo X-Stand features very well thought out design and construction along with the portability you want for the products it supports.  I do have one small gripe however, and that is the "feet" at the bottom that stabilize the laptop (by your wrists if you were typing) are a bit long.  They stick out above the edge of the iPad Pro but for MacBook Pro customers this probably wouldn't be a huge deal depending on what model/how you type.  For me personally I will probably Dremel them down a hair shorter so they're flush with the screen (I'm on the fence though as they do offer a good place to rest the Pencil) and still do their job of holding the unit in place.  In all reality the feet haven't really bothered me yet on a physical level (I've only been using it for a few days) so I guess it's more of an OCD thing.  The feet and back supports are soft polymer that provide plenty of grip/stability and I don't see them scratching any type of metal, but I use a case regardless.
If you're looking for a great way to prop up your iPad Pro for sketching/drawing/painting whatever, you've found it!  Don't go blowing all kinds of money on a more expensive stand when it's simply not necessary and lacks portability.
