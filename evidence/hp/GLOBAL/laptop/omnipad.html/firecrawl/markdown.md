## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the HP OmniPad 12?

The HP OmniPad 12 is HP’s first Android-based detachable laptop designed to combine the flexibility of a tablet with the productivity of a laptop-like experience. It features a 12-inch touchscreen display and an included detachable keyboard for flexible everyday use.



### Who is the HP OmniPad 12 designed for?

The HP OmniPad 12 is designed for first-time laptop buyers, students, families, and micro-entrepreneurs looking for an affordable, easy-to-use device for learning, work, and everyday productivity.



### What makes the HP OmniPad 12 different from a traditional tablet?

Unlike a traditional tablet, the HP OmniPad 12 is designed to deliver a laptop-like experience with features such as a taskbar, windowed multitasking, keyboard shortcuts, and support for productivity workflows, while maintaining the familiarity of Android.



### Can I use the HP OmniPad 12 as both a tablet and a laptop?

Yes. The HP OmniPad 12 features a detachable keyboard, allowing you to switch easily between tablet mode for entertainment and mobility, and a laptop-like setup for work, learning, and content creation.



### What features make the HP OmniPad 12 ideal for everyday productivity?

The HP OmniPad 12 combines Snapdragon® performance, a 12-inch 2K touchscreen display, long battery life, and a detachable keyboard with Android-based productivity features, making it well suited for everyday learning, work, and entertainment.



### What bundled apps or free subscriptions do I get with the OmniPad 12?
