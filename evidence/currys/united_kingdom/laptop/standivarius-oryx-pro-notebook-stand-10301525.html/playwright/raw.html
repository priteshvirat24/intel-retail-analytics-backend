Our FREE order & collect service

You can now order your small item(s) online, and safely collect them from your chosen store within the date of your selected delivery option.

Once you’ve selected “collect in store” at the online checkout, we’ll email you your confirmation & collection details. When you do come to collect your item(s) make sure you bring this confirmation email, and the last four digits of your payment/gift card or voucher.

Returns and refunds

If you purchased online and you have changed your mind you can return your items within 30 days even if you have opened it for inspection. It must be returned as new and where possible, in its original packaging.

If your item develops a fault we will offer either a repair, exchange or refund if the fault occurs within 30 days of purchase (or delivery or installation). If the fault with your product occurs within its guarantee period (normally 12 months from delivery) we will offer you a prompt repair service. In all cases we reserve the right to inspect the product and verify the fault.

Small items

Small item returns or refunds can be processed via stores, by contacting us or website. Items must be returned as new and where possible, in its original packaging.

Large items

Large item returns or refunds can only be processed by contacting us or website, these items cannot be returned to store. You will be contacted by a third-party courier to arrange a collection slot at your convenience. It must be returned as new and where possible, in its original packaging.

    Currys flexpay explained

    Pay at your pace

    Currys flexpay is the easy way to spread the cost of life's tech.

    Pay interest free
    Pay in monthly instalments, interest free, over terms of 12, 24 or 36 months. By paying the agreed amount every month, you won’t have to pay any interest and your balance will be paid off by the end of the promotional period.

    Pay monthly, low interest
    Spread the cost of your tech over 48 months, with a lower purchase interest rate of 9.9%. Payments are split into equal monthly instalments spanning the agreed term.

    Pay monthly
    Pay in monthly instalments for purchases £99 and over. You can choose a payment term from 12 months to 36 months. Interest is charged at your personalised interest rate.

    Buy now, pay later
    Pay as much or as little as you like over the offer period (usually 6, 9 or 12 months). If you pay the full balance of your purchase within the set period, no interest will be charged. Any balance left to pay after the set period will accrue interest from the date of purchase.

    Representative example based on the use of your ongoing credit account:
    29.9% APR Representative (variable). 29.9% Interest rate (variable). Assumed credit limit: £1,200.

    If you need a helping hand managing your Currys flexpay credit policy due to illness, accessibility needs or a change in your circumstances, we’re here to accommodate you.
